## The Event

The AYNI Hackathon was organized by the **Association of Peruvians in Silicon Valley** as part of APEC Peru 2024. The challenge: build an innovative solution for the "Smart Tourism" track in just 48 hours.

Our team -- **Sumaq Peru** -- focused on solving a real problem: tourists in Peru struggle to discover authentic experiences beyond the typical Machu Picchu route.

## The Solution

We built a platform that used AI to create personalized travel itineraries based on the traveler's interests, budget, and time constraints. Key features:

- **NLP-powered preference analysis**: travelers describe what they like, and our model matches them with curated experiences
- **Route optimization**: genetic algorithm that plans efficient multi-city trips
- **Local business integration**: small tour operators and artisans could register their services, creating economic opportunities for local communities

## The Tech Stack

We built the entire platform in 48 hours:

- **Frontend**: React with TailwindCSS for rapid prototyping
- **Backend**: Node.js with Express
- **AI**: Fine-tuned LLM for travel recommendation generation
- **Deployment**: Docker containers on a cloud VM

## The Pitch

What set us apart wasn't just the technology -- it was the narrative. We framed our solution around **sustainable tourism that benefits local communities**, not just travelers. This resonated deeply with the judges, who were looking for impact, not just code.

## What I Learned

- 48 hours forces brutal prioritization; you learn what really matters
- A compelling story beats perfect code
- Diverse teams (we had a designer, two developers, and a business student) outperform homogeneous ones
- Peruvians are incredibly welcoming -- the tourism potential is massive if channeled right
